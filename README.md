# Shopify-Sales-Analysis
 📊 Shopify Sales Analysis Dashboard

🚀 Project Overview

The Shopify Sales Analysis Dashboard is a Business Intelligence project developed using **Microsoft Excel** and **Power BI** to analyze e-commerce sales data. The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into interactive visualizations, enabling businesses to monitor sales performance, customer behavior, profitability, and regional trends for data-driven decision-making.

---

🎯 Objectives

* Analyze Shopify sales transactions and customer purchasing behavior.
* Build an interactive and user-friendly Power BI dashboard.
* Monitor revenue, profit, and sales growth trends.
* Identify top-performing and low-performing products.
* Analyze customer preferences and payment methods.
* Perform country-wise and region-wise sales analysis.
* Generate actionable business insights.

---
 🗂️ Dataset

The Kaggles dataset includes:

* Order ID
* Order Date
* Product Name
* Product Category
* Quantity Sold
* Revenue
* Profit
* Discount Offered
* Customer Country
* Payment Method
* Return Information
* Traffic Source

---
 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel** – Data Cleaning & Preparation
* **Power BI** – Dashboard Development & Visualization
* **Power Query** – Data Transformation
* **DAX** – KPI and Business Metric Calculations

---
 📈 Key Insights

* Revenue peaks during promotional campaigns and festive seasons.
* Certain product categories contribute the highest sales and profits.
* Digital payment methods are preferred by customers.
* Some regions generate a significant share of total revenue.
* Excessive discounts reduce overall profit margins.
* Product returns negatively impact profitability.
* Seasonal trends help identify peak sales periods.
